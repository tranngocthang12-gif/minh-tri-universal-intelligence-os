- identical underlying Indic wording;

- that the Chinese translations are literal mirrors of Pali;

## E. Cross-pair reasoning result

The four alignments support a hierarchy:

### HIGH-STABILITY REASONING CORES
- conditional arising/cessation;
- non-appropriation of self;
- right view as active discrimination;
- coordinated path factors;
- ethical feedback review;
- correct-vs-wrong grasp of doctrine;
- doctrine as instrument rather than identity possession.

### MEDIUM-STABILITY FORMULATIONS
- exact ordering of similes;
- exact discourse boundaries;
- elaboration of temporal/action review;
- specific narrative settings.

### LOWER-STABILITY / POSSIBLE SCHOOL SYSTEMATIZATION
- exact two-tier MN 117 path taxonomy;
- some expanded Chinese technical wording;
- local attainment notices and redactional closures.

## F. Rule added to MINH TRI

When reconstructing "Buddha's thought":
1. prefer cross-traditional functional core;
2. preserve Pali-local or Chinese-local systematization as evidence, not universal law;
3. never erase a textual anomaly;
4. distinguish shared reasoning mechanism from exact vocabulary;
5. if a later answer relies on a lower-stability formulation, disclose that dependency.
