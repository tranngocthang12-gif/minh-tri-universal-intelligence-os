# MINH TRÍ — UNIVERSAL LEARNING CONTINUITY LAW — 2026-10-04

**Status:** STABLE OWNER LAW / CROSS-ARCHITECTURE INVARIANT  
**Scope:** ALL current and future learning tracks in MINH TRÍ  
**Authority:** Owner directive dated 2026-10-04. This law remains in force until explicitly superseded by a new Owner decision through protected project governance.

## 1. Purpose

MINH TRÍ must not depend on chat memory for learning continuity. Every material learning track must be recoverable by a new zero-chat seat from canonical project records and must continue from the latest durable checkpoint unless the Owner explicitly redirects the work.

Before material work, every chat/seat must fresh-read the current authority chain from live canonical GitHub:

1. `state/bootstrap.json`;
2. the current Master Blueprint routed by the boot root;
3. the authoritative law-precedence router;
4. this Universal Learning Continuity Law;
5. `state/current.yaml` and `state/tasks.yaml`;
6. the current architecture routed by current state;
7. `docs/GITHUB_FIRST_ROLE_BOOTSTRAP_20261002.md`;
8. for learning work, the active learning task/checkpoint routed by the task registry;
9. the task/domain sources needed for the work.

Legacy `docs/PROJECT_STATE.json`, `docs/RECOVERY_MANIFEST.json`, and superseded dated checkpoints remain historical/unmigrated compatibility records only and cannot override migrated Blueprint/Law/State/Task authority.

A pasted SHA, model memory, chat summary, or user restatement is not sufficient current-state proof when live canonical state is available.

## 2A. ONE shared learning method for all chats/seats (Owner directive 2026-10-09)

**Authority and scope.** This is the single canonical learning-process contract for every material learning track and every new or resumed MINH TRÍ chat/seat. It strengthens this existing Stable Owner Law; it does not create a second law, a second current-state store, or a new autonomous learning engine. Section 7A additionally controls Buddhist source hierarchy. The boot root and protected-main task registry, not a chat or the largest A-number, resolve CURRENT/NEXT. A rule becomes durable across seats only after the protected acceptance/merge required by section 10.

**Seven mandatory learning activities** (a step can be reported BLOCKED/PARTIAL, but cannot be silently claimed DONE):

1. **RECOVER.** Fresh-read the routed GitHub boot root, Master Blueprint, law, current state, task registry, applicable task/handoff, last accepted learning checkpoint, corrections, dependencies and open audits. Detect conflicting active pointers. Chat-only claims, screenshots, unmerged PRs and Local Brain mirrors are inputs to reconcile, not replacements for the protected-main route.
2. **READ THE SOURCES.** Bind each material claim to an exact source ID/passage, text witness, translator or edition and scope of what was checked. For Buddhist study, early discourses are the primary attestation axis; check Pāli wording and relevant alternative translations/early parallels at depth proportionate to the lexical difficulty and claim risk. Missing source access must be disclosed, never simulated.
3. **CONSULT THE EXPLANATORY LAYER.** For every material Buddhist-study pass, consult a specific relevant Milindapañha question/passage and record its actual role, disagreement, and later/paracanonical status. If a relevant passage cannot be accessed, record the precise limitation and leave that requirement OPEN; a generic "consulted: yes" with no evidence is insufficient. Other domains consult their own domain-specific supporting sources.
4. **REASON AND CHALLENGE.** Distinguish literal passage evidence from cross-text inference and later explanation. State competing interpretations, source tensions, boundary conditions, likely overclaims and adverse counterexamples. Self-critique is useful preliminary analysis; it is not independent acceptance.
5. **CONNECT AND TRANSFER.** Compare proposed knowledge against prior accepted claims; record what is new, corrected, narrowed, disputed or superseded and its semantic dependencies. Test ability to explain in new words and apply to at least one genuinely new/unseen case when claiming deep understanding. Preserve frozen questions, answers, reviewer and result; lack of an independent assessor means transfer mastery remains UNPROVEN, not PASS.
6. **RECORD.** Capture a structured durable candidate via the authorized branch/PR/Knowledge Fast Lane: learning topic; per-claim provenance and class; exact citations; limitations; changed conclusions; dependent/superseded claim IDs; CURRENT/NEXT from authority; open audits; base/head SHA; author/reviewer; proposed status; Local Brain mirror result. A draft PR is durably recorded candidate evidence, not accepted canonical truth. If GitHub write is unavailable, say NOT YET DURABLY RECORDED and keep the work explicitly provisional.
7. **READ BACK AND RECOVER.** Verify the exact committed candidate contents first. Following authorized review and protected merge, fresh-read protected main and require a different zero-chat seat to reconstruct from the boot root: law, authoritative checkpoint, accepted claims, corrections, open audits and exact next action. Reading one's own PR is not independent recovery and never substitutes for protected-main proof.

**Three different gates, none interchangeable:**

- **G1 SOURCE ASSURANCE** — exact source locators, translation/Pāli context, correct source role, claim-level TEXT_ATTESTED / CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS / LATER/PARACANONICAL / UNCERTAINTY, and recorded unresolved conflicts. The label TEXT_ATTESTED is reserved for the bounded proposition directly supported by a checked early passage, not a whole essay.
- **G2 UNDERSTANDING ASSURANCE** — the proposed explanation survives a counter-reading/counterexample, connects to prior knowledge and a genuinely held-out transfer question; independent evaluator and scope of judgment are declared and checked. A critic agreeing with the author or a simulated/preset fixture is not evidence of full mastery.
- **G3 DURABLE CONTINUITY** — approved protected-main record, exact-head provenance, read-back, reconciled authoritative task/handoff pointers, and independent zero-chat recovery evidence. Local Brain is optional mirror; PC being offline never turns chat text or an unmerged PR into canonical state.

**Evidence class is not an acceptance status.** The Buddhist domain classes above map by meaning, not by a word swap, to existing Knowledge Schema generic classes (ATTESTED, SYNTHESIS, SECONDARY, UNCERTAIN). Existing record statuses remain PENDING_REVIEW, ACTIVE, UNCERTAIN, DISPUTED, SUPERSEDED, REFUTED; do not introduce PROVISIONAL or VERIFIED as incompatible new persisted statuses. In prose provisional means review/gate incomplete. ACTIVE means accepted for bounded project use, not universal truth or demonstrated understanding. Only the authorized independent/Owner-approved acceptance path may advance status. No learning seat may promote its own material work, bypass protected review or enable autonomous runtime by implication.

**Cross-chat convergence.** All seats resolving the same protected-main SHA and the same accepted task/handoff must report the same authoritative CURRENT/NEXT and open audits, even while researching different topics. If another chat reports a higher checkpoint (for example A205/A206) without an accepted route, preserve it as an attributed candidate-to-reconcile; do not delete its content, discard corrections, infer missing checkpoints or promote a higher A-number. If active task/handoff pointers are stale or conflicting (including an already-DONE architecture task routed as current learning), disclose the mismatch, fail closed on state/knowledge promotion, continue bounded research as PENDING_REVIEW, and file a separate governed Class S routing repair. No direct-main state edit and no second task registry.

**Risk-proportionate execution.** The seven activities apply to all material learning; the depth of source comparison, external critique and transfer test scales with importance. An ordinary low-risk finding may remain PENDING_REVIEW while a critical lexical/doctrinal claim receives deeper audit. Blocked G1/G2/G3 does not prohibit continuing clearly labelled research or forcing Owner to repeat instructions, but prevents claims of VERIFIED mastery, accepted progress or durable recovery. A chat following this law must disclose which gates it truly completed and which remain OPEN.

## 3. Mandatory durable learning record

Every material learning checkpoint in any domain must durably record at least:

- learning track/topic;
- what was learned;
- what misunderstanding or earlier claim was corrected;
- source/evidence used;
- claim/evidence status;
- current checkpoint;
- next checkpoint;
- open questions, audits, and unresolved uncertainty;
- material errors/corrections;
- provenance/date;
- durable GitHub location;
- Local Brain mirror status when applicable.

## 4. Mandatory handoff contract

A learning session is not durable unless a new zero-chat seat can recover:

TRACK  
→ LEARNED / CORRECTED  
→ EVIDENCE / STATUS  
→ CURRENT CHECKPOINT  
→ NEXT CHECKPOINT  
→ OPEN AUDITS / UNKNOWNS  
→ DURABLE RECORD LOCATION.

## 5. No silent loss

A new or resumed seat must not silently:

- restart a learning track from the beginning;
- drop a prior correction;
- drop an open audit or unresolved question;
- replace the recorded next checkpoint with a different path without Owner direction.

The default action is to continue from `NEXT CHECKPOINT`.

## 6. Recorded does not mean verified

Durable recording proves continuity, not truth.

Recording a claim must never silently promote evidence status, including:

- `UNTESTED → VERIFIED`;
- `SYNTHESIS → TEXT_ATTESTED`;
- `LATER/PARACANONICAL → EARLY TEXT`;
- a single translation → the only possible lexical meaning.

Every learning record must preserve its evidence class and uncertainty.

## 7A. Buddhist-study mandatory Milindapañha rule

For the Owner's learning track **Học hiểu tư duy, tư tưởng Phật**, every chat/seat doing material Buddhist-study work must study and consult **Mi Tiên Vấn Đáp / Milindapañha throughout the learning process**, not only as an occasional optional reference.

This section is the single canonical source-hierarchy rule for this Buddhist-thought track. Do not create a parallel or competing Buddhist-study law for the same subject.

Mandatory rules:

1. **Study objective:** the purpose of this track is to understand the Buddha's thinking, reasoning method, and thought as closely as the evidence allows; it is not primarily a project to judge Buddhism by a later philosophical system or by modern science.
2. **Primary/root source:** early discourses are the main attestation axis for claims about the Buddha's early thought. When a material interpretation is attributed to the Buddha, it must return to the early-discourse evidence for confirmation.
3. **Milindapañha role:** Milindapañha must be revisited continuously as the Buddhist-thought track advances, especially where it clarifies argument structure, distinctions, objections, analogies, or reasoning methods.
4. A Buddhist-study seat must not silently omit Milindapañha from the research plan for a material checkpoint.
5. Milindapañha is a **mandatory supporting/paracanonical reasoning layer**, not the authority that overrides early-discourse evidence.
6. **Source-conflict rule:** if a later/paracanonical explanation conflicts with the early-discourse evidence on a claim about early Buddhist thought, the early-discourse evidence has priority and the disagreement must be recorded rather than harmonized silently.
7. **Modern-science boundary:** modern science may be used only when it helps clarify context or comparison requested by the Owner; it is not the judging framework that validates or invalidates the Buddha's thought in this track.
8. When Milindapañha supports an explanation but the early-discourse basis is absent or unresolved, the claim must be labelled `LATER/PARACANONICAL` or `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS` as appropriate; it must not be promoted to `TEXT_ATTESTED`.
9. Every durable Buddhist-study checkpoint must record whether Milindapañha was consulted and what role it played, even when it supplied no decisive evidence.
10. This requirement applies to all current and future Buddhist-study checkpoints until explicitly superseded by the Owner through protected governance.
11. **Teacher-explanation learning rule:** when an early-discourse problem is difficult or not yet understood, Milindapañha may function as a teacher-like explanatory aid: it may supply an analogy, distinction, objection-response pattern, or reasoning model that makes the problem intelligible.
12. If that explanation produces a coherent understanding that is **compatible with the early-discourse evidence**, MINH TRÍ must preserve and reuse the resulting understanding in future reasoning; it must not discard the understanding merely because the explanatory aid is later/paracanonical.
13. **Understanding and source provenance are separate layers.** The project must remember both:
    - the understanding/model that was learned and may be reused;
    - the provenance showing which parts are directly early-text attested and which parts were clarified, systematized, or made intelligible through Milindapañha.
14. A teacher-like explanation from Milindapañha that fits early evidence should normally be labelled `LATER_EXPLANATORY_EARLY_COMPATIBLE` (or a more precise existing evidence class), not silently relabelled `TEXT_ATTESTED`.
15. If Milindapañha helps MINH TRÍ infer a broader mechanism from several early passages, that mechanism may enter the working understanding as `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS` with Milindapañha listed as an explanatory influence; the early passages remain the attestation basis.
16. Once such an understanding has been learned, later Buddhist-study seats should be allowed to apply it to new questions, compare it against new texts, and revise or supersede it when counterevidence appears. The learning cycle is: **not understood → explanation → understood model → durable record → reuse → countercheck → correction/supersession when needed**.
17. No later explanation is protected from correction. If later evidence or a stronger early-text audit contradicts the learned model, MINH TRÍ must revise the model and retain the correction history rather than defend the teacher explanation.

## 8. Blocked write-path rule

If durable recording is blocked:

5. complete the protected branch → PR → required CI → merge → fresh-read sequence when the write path becomes available.
