## 1. Durable objective

Build a continuous, source-disciplined understanding of early Buddhist thought across ChatGPT seats and chats, with special attention to dependent origination, aggregates, sense fields, feeling, craving, clinging, becoming, right view, ethics, concentration, wisdom, dispassion, liberation, anicca, dukkha, anattā, karma, continuity, rebirth, consciousness, name-and-form, and early verse material including the Aṭṭhakavagga.

The purpose is understanding and argument reconstruction, not devotional persuasion and not scientific reduction.

## 2. Source discipline

### Layer EBT — primary early-text target
Use early Nikāya/Āgama material as the first authority for claims about early Buddhist thought. Relevant targets include DN, MN, SN, AN, Sutta Nipāta/Aṭṭhakavagga, Vinaya where relevant, and parallel material in MĀ/SĀ or other early witnesses when useful.

### Layer MIL — Milindapañha / Mi Tiên Vấn Đáp
Milindapañha is an IMPORTANT DIALECTICAL SOURCE for this track. It is used to expose hidden premises, formulate hard objections, clarify continuity without an immutable self, test karma/rebirth/anattā arguments, distinguish conventional naming from analytical decomposition, and supply analogies or counterexamples.

Project rule: Milindapañha may deepen and stress-test an early-text claim, but it must not silently be relabeled as an utterance of the Buddha in the early Nikāya layer.

### Layer LATER
Later commentary or scholastic systematization may be used when helpful, but must be labeled separately. It may not overwrite EBT wording or source boundaries.

## 3. Claim labels

Use these labels when ambiguity matters:
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: directly supported by the cited primary passage.
- `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS`: synthesis across multiple primary passages.
- `MILINDAPANHA_DIALECTIC`: clarification or argument from Mi Tiên Vấn Đáp.
- `LATER_EXPOSITION`: later explanatory layer.
- `PROJECT_INTERPRETATION`: bounded reconstruction.
- `PENDING_AUDIT`: not yet source-audited enough to promote.

Never call a chat statement VERIFIED merely because it sounds coherent.

## 6. Corrections already locked into the route

1. MN 117 is used mainly to show integration/support conditions and the leading role of right view; the standard four-jhāna definition of right concentration should be sourced directly where it is actually defined, e.g. SN 45 material.
2. `dependent origination → impermanence → not-fit-to-be-taken-as-self` is a cross-text synthesis, not a verbatim single-sutta formula.
3. When source precision matters, prefer the early-text formulation that the aggregates are not fit to be regarded as "mine / I / my self" rather than turning one passage alone into an unlimited metaphysical slogan.
4. For the "same person / another person" karma problem, pair SN 12.17 with SN 12.18 rather than leaning on one passage alone.
5. MN 38 blocks the move of turning consciousness into an unchanged transmigrating entity.
6. Milindapañha analogies illuminate the logic but remain source-labeled as MIL, not EBT.
