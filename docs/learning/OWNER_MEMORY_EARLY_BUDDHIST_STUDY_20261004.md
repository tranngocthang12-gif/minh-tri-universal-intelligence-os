## Current reasoning model

The working model of early Buddhist reasoning is:

1. clarify the actual question;
2. identify the human stake beneath the wording;
3. detect false binaries and hidden metaphysical assumptions;
4. separate conditioned process from permanent-entity stories;
5. identify causal conditions and points of intervention;
6. distinguish first-order experience from reactive suffering;
7. detect appropriation: "I / me / mine";
8. audit mental roots: greed, hatred, delusion;
9. distinguish right view from grasping at views;
10. distinguish truthful knowledge from weaponized knowledge;
11. choose the proper task: understand / abandon / restrain / endure / avoid / remove / develop / realize;
12. for speech, check truth / benefit / timing;
13. for action, review before / during / after;
14. under uncertainty, compare asymmetric ethical risks without pretending certainty;
15. guard against nihilistic misreading of not-self;
16. use the minimum sufficient teaching;
17. end with something observable, trainable or testable.
