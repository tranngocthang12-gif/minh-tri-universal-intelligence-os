# EARLY BUDDHIST PARALLELS + TRANSLATION CARDS + CONTRADICTION HUNTING v0.6 — 2026-10-04

**Status:** COMPARATIVE CONSOLIDATION / CONTRADICTION LEDGER ACTIVE / NOT WHOLE-CORPUS MASTERY
**Track:** Early Buddhist Canon — Pali / English / Vietnamese / Agama-aware

## 1. Purpose

This pass deliberately tests three failure modes:
1. treating Pali wording as the only witness;
2. flattening high-risk Pali terms into one permanent English/Vietnamese gloss;
3. resolving apparent contradictions too quickly.

Method:
- identify a doctrinal claim;
- inspect relevant Pali discourse;
- inspect at least one Chinese Agama parallel where available;
- compare translation behavior;
- record contradiction status as RESOLVED / PARTIAL / UNRESOLVED;
- lower confidence when the exact technical formulation is not shared across transmission lines.

## 2. Parallel finding A — MN 22 / MA 200 / EA 43.5

MN 22 and MA 200 preserve the same broad Ariṭṭha problem:
- a monk misrepresents the teaching about sensual obstruction;
- wrong grasp of Dhamma is dangerous;
- not-self analysis is part of the corrective frame.

EA 43.5 preserves raft material as a partial parallel.

Comparative result:
- the **wrong-grasp / raft / instrumental-Dhamma core** is cross-traditional enough to treat as strong;
- exact lists, simile ordering and discourse boundaries vary;
- therefore the reasoning principle is higher-confidence than every narrative/list detail.

Evidence label:
**EARLY_MULTI_PARALLEL_HIGH_CONFIDENCE for wrong grasp / instrumental use of Dhamma.**
**EARLY_BUT_VARIANT for exact discourse composition.**

## 3. Parallel finding B — MN 61 / MA 14

MN 61 and MA 14 share:
- water-pot object lesson;
- strong condemnation of deliberate false speech;
- action review;
- purification through repeated examination.

MA 14 is more verbose in places and contains passages whose consequence wording is textually difficult/confusing in the surviving witness.

Comparative result:
- truthfulness + reflective ethical audit = strong cross-traditional core;
- exact expanded formulations in MA 14 should not be harmonized automatically.

Evidence label:
**EARLY_MULTI_PARALLEL_HIGH_CONFIDENCE for truthfulness and action-review core.**
**EARLY_BUT_VARIANT / TEXTUALLY_UNSTABLE for some expanded MA 14 wording.**

## 4. Parallel finding C — MN 117 / MA 189 / SA 785–789

MN 117 and MA 189 strongly agree on:
- right view being foremost;
- right view, right effort and right mindfulness working together around other path factors;
- rejection of moral nihilist wrong view;
- right path factors as a coordinated training architecture.

Important divergence:
- MN 117 gives an explicit technical distinction between a right-view/path-factor form associated with merit/defilements and a noble, undefiled, transcendent form;
- the direct Chinese/Tibetan parallels do not preserve this exact two-level analysis in the same way;
- similar transcendent-factor analyses do occur in SA 785/789.

Comparative result:
- **coordinated path architecture** = strong;
- **exact two-tier technical scheme in MN 117** = lower historical confidence than the shared core.

Evidence label:
**EARLY_MULTI_PARALLEL_HIGH_CONFIDENCE for path coordination.**
**SCHOOL_SPECIFIC_POSSIBLE / EARLY_BUT_VARIANT for exact two-level technical formulation.**

## 5. Parallel finding D — SN 12.15 / SA 301

SN 12.15 and SA 301 closely preserve:
- right view;
- rejection of existence/non-existence extremes;
- grasping/appropriation as central;
- "only suffering arises / only suffering ceases" style de-appropriation;
- dependent arising/cessation as the middle teaching.

Comparative result:
The **anti-binary conditional structure** is not merely a late Pali peculiarity.

Evidence label:
**EARLY_MULTI_PARALLEL_HIGH_CONFIDENCE.**

## 6. Translation card — sammadiṭṭhi / 正見 / chánh kiến / right view

### Risk
One permanent gloss hides major contextual variation.

### SN 12.15 context
Right view means:
- seeing arising and cessation;
- not falling into existence/non-existence absolutization;
- not fastening on "my self";
- understanding suffering conditionally.

### MN 117 / MA 189 context
Right view also includes:
- recognizing wrong view as wrong;
- affirming moral causality and ethical consequence;
- guiding effort and mindfulness.

### Translation rule
Keep "right view / chánh kiến" as the label, but always attach a **context card**:
- CONDITIONAL-NONREIFICATION;
- ETHICAL-CAUSAL;
- PATH-DISCRIMINATIVE;
- NOBLE/TRANSCENDENT when textually warranted.

Never infer that one definition exhausts all occurrences.

## 7. Translation card — upadana / 取 / chấp thủ / clinging

Possible functions:
- grasping;
- taking up;
- appropriation;
- identity investment;
- taking a view/practice/sensuality/self-doctrine as a basis.

Risk:
"chấp thủ" can sound purely intellectual in Vietnamese.
"clinging" can sound merely emotional in English.

Operational gloss:
**appropriative grasping that supplies a basis for identity/continuation.**

Do not force this full gloss into every sentence; preserve the Pali in technical analysis.

## 8. Translation card — atta me / 計我 / "my self" / "my identity"

SN 12.15 is a high-risk case.

Possible English:
- "my self";
- "my identity."

Vietnamese can easily over-metaphysicalize this into a universal theorem about whether a self exists.

Chinese SA 301's wording around 計我 strengthens the sense of:
- reckoning/conceiving in terms of self.

Operational rule:
In this context translate functionally:
**the mind does not take/establish the process as "self/mine".**

Do not use the line as a standalone proof of a maximal metaphysical thesis without context.

## 9. Translation card — dhamma in the raft/snake context

"Dhamma / Pháp" may refer to:
- teachings;
- doctrinal formulations;
- phenomena;
- qualities/principles.

In MN 22's raft frame, the operative sense is primarily:
**teaching as instrument for crossing**, not an assertion that every occurrence of dhamma is disposable doctrine.

Risk:
"abandon the Dhamma" can be misread as:
- truth is irrelevant;
- right view is unnecessary;
- doctrine has no valid content.

Corrective:
What is relinquished is **grasping/carrying the instrument beyond its function**, not disciplined discernment while crossing.

## 10. Translation card — attha / atthasaṁhita in MN 58

Common renderings:
- beneficial;
- useful;
- connected with benefit;
- meaningful/purposeful.

Risk:
"Useful" may become subjective convenience.
"Beneficial" may become merely pleasant.

Operational meaning:
**conducive to welfare/training in the pedagogical-ethical context.**

Speech gate remains:
- true;
- beneficial in this disciplined sense;
- timely.

## 11. Translation card — paccavekkhati in MN 61

Common range:
- review;
- reflect;
- examine;
- repeatedly inspect.

The mirror image and before/during/after structure make a one-time "reflection" too weak.

Operational gloss:
**iterative reflective audit.**

Vietnamese working gloss:
**quán xét / phản tỉnh lặp lại trước–trong–sau hành động.**

## 12. Translation card — sabba in SN 35.23

Literal label:
- "the all" / "tất cả".

Risk:
reading this automatically as:
- a final metaphysical denial of any external world;
- modern idealism.

Operational rule:
First read it as a bounded discourse about the complete **sense-cognitive domain accessible to the teaching's analysis**.
Any ontological extension beyond that requires separate argument.

## 13. Contradiction ledger C01 — raft vs right view

### Surface contradiction
MN 22: even Dhamma is like a raft not to be carried.
MN 117 / MA 189: right view is foremost and actively cultivated.

### Bad harmonization
"Right view does not matter."

### Resolution
Distinguish:
- **functional right view** as an indispensable path instrument;
- **appropriative grasping** at doctrine as identity/status/weapon.

The same teaching can be:
- necessary for crossing;
- harmful if grasped wrongly.

Status:
**RESOLVED AT FUNCTION/GRASPING LEVEL.**

## 14. Contradiction ledger C02 — anti-existence binary vs affirmative right view

### Surface contradiction
SN 12.15 / SA 301 reject the extremes:
- everything exists;
- everything does not exist.

MN 117 / MA 189 affirm right-view propositions such as:
- moral action has consequence;
- this world/another world;
- mother/father;
- realized practitioners.

### Bad harmonization
"The Buddha rejects all propositional truth claims."

### Resolution
The contexts target different errors:
- SN 12.15 rejects **reified ontological absolutization** and replaces it with conditional arising/cessation;
- MN 117 / MA 189 reject **moral/causal nihilism** and provide action-guiding discrimination.

Thus:
**anti-reification is not anti-assertion.**

Status:
**RESOLVED CONTEXTUALLY, WITH ONGOING METAPHYSICAL CAUTION.**

## 15. Contradiction ledger C03 — anti-view-grasping vs right view

### Surface contradiction
Aṭṭhakavagga/MN 22 warn against clinging to views and doctrine.
The path requires right view.

### Resolution
Distinguish:
- epistemically/ethically correct discrimination;
- identity-forming possession of a view.

Right view guides release.
View-grasping converts cognition into self/contest.

Status:
**RESOLVED BY CONTENT/APPROPRIATION DISTINCTION.**

## 16. Contradiction ledger C04 — "the all" vs cosmological/rebirth discourse

### Surface contradiction
SN 35.23 defines "the all" through six sense domains.
Elsewhere early discourses speak of devas, rebirth and multiple realms.

### Premature resolution to avoid
- "Therefore devas are only mental states."
- "Therefore SN 35.23 proves metaphysical idealism."
- "Therefore the texts are simply inconsistent."

### Current assessment
SN 35.23 is best read first as delimiting the analyzable/knowable sense-cognitive field for the discourse.
How this relates to cosmological claims needs a larger corpus and parallel audit.

Status:
**PARTIAL / UNRESOLVED AT METAPHYSICAL LEVEL.**

## 17. Contradiction ledger C05 — consequence audit vs rule-like ethics

### Surface contradiction
MN 61 evaluates action through harm before/during/after.
Other passages strongly prohibit deliberate false speech and specify right speech/action.

### Resolution
Early ethics contains both:
- constitutive constraints/virtues;
- consequence-sensitive reflective feedback.

It is neither:
- pure rule deontology;
nor
- simple act consequentialism.

Status:
**RESOLVED AS MULTI-LAYER ETHICS.**

## 18. Contradiction ledger C06 — Pali exact technical scheme vs parallel absence

### Surface contradiction
MN 117's explicit two-tier right-view/path-factor formulation is technically elaborate.
MA 189/Up 6.080 do not preserve the exact same distinction, while SA 785/789 preserve related analysis.

### Correct response
Do not force textual identity.
Downgrade confidence for:
- exact wording;
- exact systematization;
while retaining higher confidence for the shared coordinated-path core.

Status:
**TEXTUAL DIVERGENCE ACCEPTED / NOT HARMONIZED.**

## 19. Contradiction ledger C07 — MA 14 expanded wording difficulty

MA 14 and MN 61 strongly agree on the Rāhula ethical-audit core.
But some expanded MA 14 passages have difficult consequence wording in the surviving Chinese witness.

Correct response:
- preserve the strong shared core;
- mark the problematic local wording;
- do not invent a hidden meaning to save perfect consistency.

Status:
**LOCAL TEXTUAL PROBLEM / UNRESOLVED.**

## 20. New reasoning safeguards

Add to the Buddha-reasoning model:

- actively search for a discourse that appears to say the opposite.

- label it;
- lower confidence;
- continue research.

- which reasoning pattern is shared?
- which technical wording may be school/transmission specific?
