## 1. SN 12 — dependent origination as diagnostic grammar

### Anchor A — SN 12.2 Vibhaṅga
Role:
- defines the standard dependent-origination sequence;
- defines individual links rather than merely reciting a chain.

Reasoning pattern:
**DEFINE THE PROCESS BEFORE THE THEORY.**

Important implication:
Dependent origination should not be treated merely as a diagram to memorize. Its links have local meanings and must be understood in their discourse context.

Translation risks:
- avijjā;
- saṅkhāra;
- viññāṇa;
- nāmarūpa;
- bhava.

Guard:
Do not force one three-life interpretation into every use of dependent origination.

### Anchor B — SN 12.15 Kaccānagotta
Role:
- right view;
- existence/non-existence extremes;
- self-reckoning;
- conditional arising/cessation.

Reasoning pattern:
**REPLACE REIFIED BINARY WITH CONDITIONAL ANALYSIS.**

High-confidence cross-traditional support:
SĀ 301 preserves the same core structure.

### Anchor C — SN 12.23 Upanisā
Role:
- links the arising of suffering with the conditional unfolding of the path toward liberation.

Important sequence:
suffering -> confidence/faith -> gladness -> rapture -> tranquility -> happiness -> samādhi -> knowledge-and-vision-as-it-is -> disenchantment -> dispassion -> freedom -> knowledge of ending.

Reasoning pattern:
**THE PATH ITSELF IS CONDITIONED.**

New insight:
The teaching does not merely analyze the causal production of suffering. It also analyzes the causal production of liberation.

Guard:
Do not interpret "conditioned path" as meaning liberation is mechanically guaranteed. Conditions must be cultivated.

### Anchor D — SN 12.38 Cetanā
Role:
- intention/planning/preoccupation;
- establishment of consciousness;
- renewed existence.

Reasoning pattern:
**MENTAL ACTIVITY IS CAUSALLY GENERATIVE.**

Guard:
Continuity is not proof of an unchanged consciousness-substance.

## 2. SN 22 — aggregates as anti-appropriation analysis

### Anchor A — SN 22.59 Anattalakkhaṇa
Argument structure:
1. if aggregate were self, it should not lead to affliction in the given way;
2. it should be subject to sovereign control;
3. it is impermanent and changing;
4. what is impermanent and afflicted is unfit to regard as "mine / I am this / this is my self";
5. correct seeing leads to disenchantment -> dispassion -> release.

Reasoning pattern:
**TEST SELF-APPROPRIATION RATHER THAN MERELY ASSERT "NO SELF".**

Vietnamese translation check:
HT Thích Minh Châu preserves:
"Cái này không phải của tôi, cái này không phải là tôi, cái này không phải tự ngã của tôi."

### Anchor B — SN 22.85 Yamaka
Problem:
not-self is misread as:
"the liberated person is annihilated and ceases to exist after death."

Correction:
the person cannot be located as identical with, inside, outside, equal to, or independent from the aggregates in the naive manner presupposed by the annihilation claim.

Reasoning pattern:
**REMOVE THE ENTITY PREMISE BEFORE DISCUSSING ITS DESTRUCTION.**

Guard:
Anattā must carry an anti-annihilation check.

### Anchor C — grasping aggregate family
Working distinction:
- aggregate = analytical category;
- grasping aggregate = aggregate taken up through appropriation/clinging.

Reasoning update:
The central problem is not the mere occurrence of form/feeling/perception/formations/consciousness, but the relation of appropriation to them.

## 3. SN 35 — the sense interface

### Anchor A — SN 35.23 Sabba
Defines "the all" using six sense faculties and corresponding domains.

Reasoning pattern:
**BOUND THE ANALYTICAL DOMAIN TO EXPERIENCE ACCESSIBLE THROUGH THE SENSE-MIND SYSTEM.**

Guard:
Do not automatically infer modern metaphysical idealism.

Open contradiction:
SN 35.23 vs cosmological/rebirth discourse remains partial/unresolved at the metaphysical level.

### Anchor B — SN 35.28 Ādittapariyāya
The six sense fields, consciousness, contact, and feeling are described as burning with greed, hatred, delusion, and the suffering associated with conditioned existence.

Reasoning pattern:
**THE PROBLEM IS NOT ONLY WHAT IS PERCEIVED, BUT HOW THE ENTIRE CONTACT SYSTEM IS AFFECTIVELY IGNITED.**

New synthesis:
perception is not a neutral camera feed followed only later by optional emotion.
Contact/feeling/affective investment are structurally central.

### Anchor C — SN 35.30 and conceiving family
Links the six-sense domain with recurring conceiving/appropriation patterns also seen in MN 1.

Reasoning pattern:
**EXPERIENCE -> CONCEIVING -> APPROPRIATION.**

Guard:
Do not flatten maññati/conceiving into ordinary "thinking."

## 4. SN 36 — feeling as a causal hinge

### Anchor A — SN 36.6 Sallatha
Both ordinary and trained persons experience pleasant, painful and neutral feeling.

The distinction:
- first pain/feeling;
- second reactive layer of mental distress, resistance and craving.

Reasoning pattern:
**DO NOT DENY PRIMARY PAIN; TRAIN THE SECOND REACTION.**

Guard:
This is not "pain is unreal."

### Anchor B — SN 36.23
Defines:
- three feelings;
- feeling arising with contact;
- craving as the path that intensifies/continues the problem;
- cessation with cessation of contact;
- Noble Eightfold Path as the practice leading to cessation;
- gratification, drawback and escape regarding feeling.

Reasoning pattern:
**ORIGIN / GRATIFICATION / DRAWBACK / ESCAPE.**

New synthesis:
Vedanā is not merely a passive datum.
It is one of the central pivot points where craving can either arise or be understood.

## 5. SN 45 — the path as coordinated intervention

### Anchor A — SN 45.8 Vibhaṅga
Defines all eight path factors.

Reasoning pattern:
**A PRACTICAL SYSTEM MUST SPECIFY ITS COMPONENT OPERATIONS.**

Important:
right view is not detached philosophy; the definitions place it inside a trainable system.

### Cross-check — MN 117 / MĀ 189
Strengthens:

- right effort and right mindfulness work around the correction of other factors;
- path factors coordinate rather than operate as isolated checklist items.

Guard:

## 6. SN 46 — awakening factors as dynamic regulation

### Anchor A — SN 46.3 Sīla
A practitioner:
- hears/recalls the teaching;
- mindfulness is activated;
- investigates Dhamma;
- energy arises;
- rapture;
- tranquility;
- samādhi;
- equanimity.

Reasoning pattern:
**AWAKENING FACTORS FORM A DYNAMIC CASCADE, NOT A STATIC LIST.**

Important correction:
sati can include recollection/keeping the teaching in mind, not only bare present-moment awareness.

### Anchor B — broader SN 46 balancing principle
Across the awakening-factor material:
- some factors energize;
- some calm;
- mindfulness remains broadly useful;
- development depends on the state of mind and on wise attention.

Reasoning pattern:
**MATCH THE MENTAL MEDICINE TO THE MENTAL STATE.**

This converges with MN 2's method-selection architecture.

### Anchor C — MN 118 cross-system integration
Mindfulness of breathing -> satipaṭṭhāna -> awakening factors -> knowledge and freedom.

Reasoning pattern:
**PRACTICES NEST AND FEED ONE ANOTHER.**
