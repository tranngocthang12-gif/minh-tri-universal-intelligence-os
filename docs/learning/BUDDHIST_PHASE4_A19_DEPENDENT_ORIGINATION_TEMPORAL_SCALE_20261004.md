# PHASE 4 — A19 — DEPENDENT ORIGINATION ACROSS PRESENT EXPERIENCE AND REBIRTH

Status: CHAT_TAUGHT / PARTIAL_SOURCE_AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-04

Question: Does dependent origination operate only in present-moment psychology, or is it a multi-life rebirth structure? Can both readings be preserved without forcing one reductive scheme?

## SN 12.2 / SN 12.3
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the standard sequence includes ignorance → formations → consciousness → name-and-form → six sense fields → contact → feeling → craving → grasping → continued existence → rebirth → aging-and-death.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: SN 12.3 explicitly presents the arising sequence as wrong practice and the cessation sequence as right practice.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: dependent origination is therefore not merely a speculative cosmology; the discourses tie it to practice and cessation.

## SN 12.38 and SN 12.40 — intention, underlying tendencies, and future rebirth
- TEXT_ATTESTED: what one intends, plans, and has underlying tendencies toward becomes a basis/support for the continuation or establishment of consciousness.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: when consciousness is established and grows, the texts continue through inclination, coming-and-going or name-and-form depending on the discourse variant, and explicitly to future renewed existence/rebirth, aging-and-death, and suffering.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: when intention/planning/underlying tendency no longer provide that basis, consciousness is not established in that way and future rebirth/suffering ceases.
- SOURCE DISCIPLINE: these passages make a future-life/rebirth dimension explicit; they should not be reduced to a purely one-moment psychology.

## SN 12.52 and SN 12.64
- TEXT_ATTESTED: focusing on gratification in things that fuel grasping grows craving → grasping → continued existence → rebirth → aging-and-death; focusing on drawbacks supports the cessation sequence.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: where passion, delight, and craving for nutriment are present, consciousness is established/grows and renewed existence develops; where they are absent, that sequence does not develop.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: present acts of attention, craving, and appropriation are described as causally relevant to future becoming/rebirth.

## SN 12.23
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the same conditional grammar is used to connect suffering to faith, joy, tranquility, concentration, truly knowing-and-seeing, disenchantment, dispassion, freedom, and knowledge of ending.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: dependent conditionality is a general explanatory grammar, not only a fixed twelve-box timeline.

## MN 18 / SN 35 bridge
- TEXT_ATTESTED: sense contact, feeling, perception, thinking, and proliferative elaboration can be analyzed within present experience; SN 35 materials likewise trace bondage and release through current sensory processes.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: the early texts clearly apply conditional analysis to processes observable within lived experience without thereby erasing the explicit rebirth language elsewhere.

## Main reconciliation
CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS:
The safest reading at this stage is not "dependent origination is only momentary psychology" and not "dependent origination is only a rigid three-life chart". The early discourses use dependent conditionality at more than one temporal scale: present sensory/cognitive processes, present craving and grasping, ongoing becoming, and explicitly future rebirth. The later three-life allocation may be a useful systematization, but it should not be silently projected back as the only meaning of every early passage.

## Milindapañha dialectical layer
- MILINDAPANHA_DIALECTIC: Mi Tiên's continuity analogies help test how causation can link temporally separated phases without requiring an invariant substance-person.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: this is useful for understanding how present intention/craving can be causally connected with future rebirth while preserving anattā.
- Guardrail: Mi Tiên clarifies logical continuity; it does not prove that every link of the standard dependent-origination formula must be assigned to a fixed three-life map.

## Guardrails
- Do not collapse `jāti` in every standard dependent-origination passage into a purely psychological "birth of an identity" when passages explicitly speak of future rebirth.
- Do not deny present experiential applications merely because the sequence includes rebirth.
- Do not claim the early texts present one explicit, exhaustive "three-life interpretation" identical with later manuals.
- Do not force every discourse into one temporal scale; preserve passage-specific wording.
