# AṬṬHAKAVAGGA LEXICAL PASS v0.3 — 2026-10-04

**Status:** FIRST LEXICAL CONTROL PASS / NOT FULL PHILOLOGICAL EDITION
**Terms:** diṭṭhi, sacca, saññā, maññati-family, papañca, nissaya

## 1. diṭṭhi — view

Risk:
Translate every occurrence simply as "opinion" or every occurrence as a fully technical wrong-view category.

In Aṭṭhakavagga the recurrent problem is often not bare cognition but:
- adopting a view as identity;
- claiming superiority;
- defining purity through it;
- entering dispute through attachment to it.

Working range:
- view;
- doctrinal position;
- stance;
- grasped identity-position.

Control:
Do not infer:
"all views are rejected."
Compare with right-view material in MN 117/SN 45.

## 2. sacca — truth

High-risk tension:
Snp 4.12 criticizes competing groups each proclaiming their own truth, yet the text also invokes the singularity of truth.

Therefore:
- anti-dogmatism ≠ relativism;
- multiple rival claims do not imply there is no truth.

Working rule:
distinguish:
- truth/reality;
- proposition claimed as true;
- identity built around "my truth."

## 3. saññā — perception / recognition / designation

Risk:
"perception" in English may sound like raw passive sensing.
Aṭṭhakavagga's conflict analysis can involve:
- recognition;
- categorization;
- pleasant/unpleasant differentiation;
- conceptual discrimination tied to contact and desire.

Working rule:
Use "perception/recognition" and specify function.

Do not translate saññā as:
- full conceptual thought;
nor
- bare sensation.

## 4. maññati / conceiving family

The `maññati` family is central in MN 1 and related anti-appropriation analysis.
In Aṭṭhakavagga, similar **conceiving/comparison/identity-making functions** appear, but the chapter should not be mechanically rewritten using MN 1's exact formula.

Working rule:
- track actual occurrence where present;
- use "conceive / imagine / reckon" contextually;
- do not use one lemma to erase genre differences.

Key conceptual function:
turning experience or a stance into:
- "I am";
- "mine";
- "higher/equal/lower";
- a basis for contest.

## 5. papañca — proliferation / objectifying elaboration

High-risk term.

Secure function:
- mental elaboration connected with conflict;
- multiplication of conceptual distinctions;
- a process that can feed quarrel and appropriation.

Unresolved:
No single English/Vietnamese gloss captures all uses.

Working translation:
- keep `papañca` when precision matters;
- explain as "tăng sinh/phóng diễn khái niệm có tính chấp thủ/xung đột" where needed.

Do not equate the word lexically with:
- craving alone;
- conceit alone;
- views alone,
even if later commentary relates these.

## 6. nissaya — support / dependence / reliance

In anti-grasping contexts, nissaya can point to what a person:
- relies on;
- rests on;
- uses as a support for identity/security.

Working range:
- support;
- basis;
- dependence;
- reliance.

Reasoning importance:
A sage may be described as not taking a doctrinal or experiential support as an identity-ground.

Guard:
"not relying" does not mean:
- no causal conditions;
- no teachers;
- no path;
- no practical supports.

It targets a mode of existential/identity dependence in context.

## 7. Cross-term synthesis

Aṭṭhakavagga repeatedly traces a pattern:

CONTACT / EXPERIENCE
-> SAÑÑĀ / DIFFERENTIATION
-> DESIRE / DEARNESS
-> CONCEIVING / COMPARISON
-> DIṬṬHI OR PURITY IDENTITY
-> NISSAYA / RELIANCE
-> PAPAÑCA / CONFLICT

This is a **working causal synthesis**, not a claim that every sutta states one identical chain.

## 8. Major interpretive guard

The chapter should not be reduced to:
- skepticism;
- nihilism;
- anti-intellectualism;
- "have no beliefs."

Better:
**do not turn cognitive content into possessive identity and conflict.**

Positive discipline remains present:
- restraint;
- non-violence;
- speech control;
- endurance;
- non-sensuality;
- composure.
