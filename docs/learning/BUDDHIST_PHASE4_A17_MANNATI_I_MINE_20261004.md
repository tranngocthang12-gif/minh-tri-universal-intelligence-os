# PHASE 4 — A17 — MAÑÑATI: HOW EXPERIENCE BECOMES "I / MINE"

Status: CHAT_TAUGHT / PARTIAL_SOURCE_AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-04

Question: How does `maññati` / conceiving transform experienced phenomena into "mine", self-reference, and clinging without requiring a permanent self-substance?

## MN 1 Mūlapariyāya
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the uninstructed person perceives a phenomenon as that phenomenon, then conceives in relation to it in several modes, conceives it as "mine", and delights in it.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the trainee directly knows the phenomenon and is instructed not to conceive in those ways, not to conceive it as "mine", and not to delight in it.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the arahant directly knows the phenomenon but does not conceive in those modes or as "mine"; the discourse connects this with full comprehension and the ending of passion, aversion, and delusion.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the same pattern is extended even to Nibbāna/Unbinding: it too can be conceived as "mine" by one who has not comprehended.
- GUARDRAIL: `maññati` here should not be reduced to "thinking" or ordinary conceptual recognition. The arahant still directly knows "earth as earth"; what ceases is the conceiving/appropriative overlay.

## SN 35.30 — bounded SN 35 audit slice
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the discourse presents a way for uprooting all conceivings across eye, forms, eye-consciousness, contact, and feeling, and likewise across the other sense domains.
- TEXT_ATTESTED: the practitioner does not conceive the item, in it, from it, or "it is mine".
- TEXT_ATTESTED: not conceiving in this way leads to not clinging; not clinging leads to non-agitation and personal realization of Nibbāna.
- AUDIT BOUNDARY: this closes only a bounded SN 35.30 slice for the A17 question. The full SN 35 ID-level backlog remains open.

## SN 35.191
- TEXT_ATTESTED: neither the eye nor forms are the fetter; desire-passion arising dependent on the two is the fetter.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: liberation is not achieved by abolishing objects or sensory capacities, but by ending the appropriative/desire-passion relation that turns contact into bondage.

## Snp 4.6 Jarā
- TEXT_ATTESTED / lexical: what a person construes as "mine" is left behind at death; grief and possessiveness are tied to what is taken as "mine".
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: `mine-making` converts impermanent experience into a possession claim that necessarily collides with separation and loss.

## Snp 4.11 Kalahavivāda
- TEXT_ATTESTED / lexical: `saññānidānā hi papañcasaṅkhā` links `papañcasaṅkhā` with perception as condition/source.
- SOURCE CAUTION: `papañca` has a disputed semantic range across translations and scholarship. Do not identify it simplistically with "thinking too much", and do not treat it as a synonym for `maññati`.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: the Aṭṭhakavagga supports examining how perception can become elaborated into classifications, preference, conceit, and conflict, but the exact lexical mechanism must remain source-bounded.

## SN 22.89 bridge
- TEXT_ATTESTED: even when no aggregate is taken doctrinally as self or belonging to self, a residual "I am" conceit/desire/tendency can remain.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: this supports reading `maññati` as a subtler self-referential/appropriative process than explicit self-theory alone.

## Milindapañha dialectical layer
- MILINDAPANHA_DIALECTIC: the chariot/Nāgasena argument preserves conventional designation while denying the need for a permanent person-substance.
- CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: therefore ending self-conceiving does not require destroying ordinary naming or functional recognition. "Nāgasena" and "chariot" can remain useful designations without being reified into an independent essence.
- Guardrail: later Milindapañha formulations about ultimate/conventional language should not be silently projected back into MN 1 as if identical in wording or theory.

## Main conclusion
CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS:
MN 1 and SN 35.30 show a crucial distinction between knowing an experience and appropriating/conceiving around it. The problem is not bare perception or the capacity to identify a phenomenon; it is the overlay that construes in relation to it, makes it "mine", delights in it, and thereby supports clinging. This explains how "I / mine" can be constructed as a process without requiring a permanent self-substance.

## Guardrails
- Do not translate `maññati` simply as all conceptual thought.
- Do not say the arahant becomes unable to recognize ordinary objects or use conventional language.
- Do not equate `maññati`, `asmimāna`, and `papañca`; they overlap in the analytical field of selfing/elaboration but are not synonyms.
- Do not infer that sensory objects themselves are bondage; SN 35.191 locates the fetter in desire-passion arising dependent on the pair.
