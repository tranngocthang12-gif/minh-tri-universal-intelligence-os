# PHASE 4 — A25 — FORGIVENESS, TRUST, BOUNDARIES, AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Status: CHAT_TAUGHT / PARTIAL_SOURCE_AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-04

Question: If hatred is abandoned, what remains of accountability, memory, prudence, trust, and boundaries?

MN 21: non-hatred is trained even under severe provocation. This concerns the quality of mind; it does not state that memory must be erased, trust restored, or contact continued.

MN 61: bodily, verbal, and mental action are examined before, during, and after for harm to oneself, others, or both. Harmful conduct is to be stopped, acknowledged, clarified, and restrained in future. This preserves correction and responsibility.

MN 58: speech is assessed by truth, benefit, and timing, not pleasantness alone. Wrongdoing can therefore be named without requiring malice.

AN 3.27: a person prone to anger and harm may be regarded with equanimity and not associated with, followed, or served. Equanimity and distance can coexist.

DN 31: false friends are to be recognized and kept at a distance; trustworthy friends protect, advise, and remain in adversity. Goodwill does not require indiscriminate trust.

AN 5.57: beings are owners and heirs of their deeds. This preserves deed/consequence responsibility, but it is not by itself a complete modern legal theory.

MILINDAPANHA_DIALECTIC: Nāgasena preserves karmic responsibility without an immutable self by causal continuity: neither strictly the same nor wholly another. The mango, lamp, and name-and-form arguments stress-test how responsibility can continue without a permanent ego-substance.

CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS: abandoning hatred is different from restoring trust. Trust is a judgment about reliability; boundaries are prudential limits; accountability remains because harmful conduct still has consequences and calls for correction.

Useful distinction: non-hatred = no wish to injure; memory = do not pretend the event did not occur; trust = confidence only where conduct supports it; boundary = limit access where risk remains; accountability = harmful action still calls for correction and consequence.

Guardrails: do not turn non-hatred into compulsory reconciliation; do not equate forgiveness with restored trust; do not use prudence as a disguise for revenge or contempt; do not infer a full modern legal theory from karma passages.

Bounded audit progress: MN 21, MN 61, MN 58, AN 3.27, DN 31, AN 5.57. Wider collection-level audits remain open.
