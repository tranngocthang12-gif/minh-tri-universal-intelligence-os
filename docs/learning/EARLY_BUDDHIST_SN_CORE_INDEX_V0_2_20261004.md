## 1. SN 12 — Nidāna-saṃyutta

### Structural core
SN 12 is not merely a twelve-link cosmological chart.

The indexed material supports at least five functional uses:

1. **Causal analysis of suffering**
   - ignorance -> choices/saṅkhāra -> consciousness -> name-and-form -> six sense fields -> contact -> feeling -> craving -> grasping -> continued existence -> rebirth -> aging/death/distress.

2. **Cessation analysis**
   - the same structure is reversible;
   - cessation is treated as practical path logic, not merely speculative philosophy.

3. **Frame correction**
   - SN 12.15: existence/non-existence duality is replaced with conditioned arising and cessation;
   - SN 12.12: "who?" questions that presuppose a carrier-self are replaced with "under what conditions?"

4. **Continuity without substance**
   - SN 12.38 links intention/planning with establishment and renewed existence;
   - this supports causal continuity but does not license an unchanged consciousness-self.

5. **Path as conditioned development**
   - SN 12.23 extends conditionality from suffering into the path:
     suffering -> faith -> joy -> rapture -> tranquility -> bliss -> samādhi -> knowing/seeing -> disillusionment -> dispassion -> freedom.

### Anchor discourses indexed
- SN 12.3 — dependent origination/cessation explicitly framed as wrong/right practice;
- SN 12.12 — "who?" replaced with conditionality;
- SN 12.15 — right view and existence/non-existence extremes;
- SN 12.20 — conditionality as structural principle;
- SN 12.23 — path-development chain as conditional sequence;
- SN 12.38 — intention, consciousness establishment, renewed existence;
- SN 12.44 — "world" arising via sense consciousness -> contact -> feeling -> craving -> grasping;
- DN 15 retained as a cross-collection stress witness for consciousness/name-and-form.

### Reasoning architecture extracted
- CONDITIONAL;
- FRAME_REPLACEMENT;
- WHO_TO_CONDITION_REFRAME;
- CAUSAL_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_SUBSTANCE;
- PATH_AS_CONDITIONED_DEVELOPMENT.

### Translation risks
- paṭiccasamuppāda;
- saṅkhāra;
- viññāṇa;
- nāmarūpa;
- upādāna;
- bhava;
- jāti;
- upanisā.

### Current contradiction links
- C02: anti-existence binary vs affirmative ethical-causal right view;
- C10: same-consciousness error vs womb-consciousness language;
- hard cluster v0.8: rebirth mechanism remains partially underdetermined.

### Phase-1 result

## 2. SN 22 — Khandha-saṃyutta

### Structural core
SN 22 is best modeled as an analysis of:
- what is appropriated;
- how self-view forms;
- how anxiety follows grasping;
- how de-appropriation functions;
- why not-self must not be turned into annihilationism.

### Anchor discourses indexed
- SN 22.8 — grasping "mine / I am this / my self" generates anxiety;
- SN 22.17 — not-self formula applied to the five aggregates;
- SN 22.47 — self-views are mapped through the five grasping aggregates;
- SN 22.59 — impermanence, affliction/lack of control, and fitness for appropriation;
- SN 22.79 — even recollection of past lives is articulated through aggregate processes;
- SN 22.82 — scope and immediate conditions of aggregates;
- SN 22.85 — Yamaka: not-self cannot be converted into "a real being is annihilated";
- SN 22.125 and related material — freedom from I-making, mine-making and conceit through non-grasping.

### Reasoning architecture extracted
- DE_APPROPRIATIVE;
- CONTROL_TEST;
- ANXIETY_FROM_GRASPING;
- ANTI_NIHILISM;
- PROCESS_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_SELF_ESSENCE.

### Important synthesis
The aggregate teaching is not merely a taxonomy.
Its recurring task is:
**identify what is being taken as self -> inspect instability/control -> release appropriation.**

### Translation risks
- khandha / upādānakkhandha;
- attā;
- etaṁ mama / eso'hamasmi / eso me attā;
- saññā;
- saṅkhāra;
- viññāṇa;
- māna / asmimāna.

### Current contradiction links
- SN 22.85 annihilation guard;
- Aṭṭhakavagga/right-view distinction;
- rebirth continuity vs permanent identity.

## 3. SN 35 — Saḷāyatana-saṃyutta

### Structural core
SN 35 provides the primary interface model of experience:
- sense faculty;
- object;
- consciousness;
- contact;
- feeling;
- perception/intention;
- craving/grasping;
- bondage or release.

### Anchor discourses indexed
- SN 35.23 — "the all" as eye/forms ... mind/mental phenomena;
- SN 35.28 — sense fields "burning" with greed, hate, delusion and suffering;
- SN 35.93 — consciousness arises dependent on a duality; faculty/object/consciousness/contact are impermanent;
- SN 35.118 — approval/welcoming/clinging to attractive sense objects gives consciousness support/fuel for grasping;
- SN 12.44 cross-link — origin/disappearance of "world" analyzed through sense consciousness, contact, feeling, craving and grasping.

### Reasoning architecture extracted
- EXPERIENTIAL_DOMAIN_DISCIPLINE;
- CONDITION_DEPENDENT_CONSCIOUSNESS;
- CONTACT_TO_FEELING;
- APPROVAL_TO_GRASPING;
- ANTI_ONTOLOGICAL_OVERREACH.

### Important synthesis
SN 35 prevents two opposite mistakes:
1. turning consciousness into an independent witness;
2. treating sensory cognition as a neutral camera feed detached from affective/appropriative dynamics.

### Translation risks

- saññā;

- nandi / abhinandati;
- upādāna.

## 4. SN 36 — Vedanā-saṃyutta

### Structural core
Feeling is a causal hinge, not merely "emotion."

The indexed material distinguishes:
- pleasant;
- painful;
- neutral feeling;
- contact as immediate condition;
- reaction patterns;
- sensual vs renunciant/non-sensual feeling;
- gratification, drawback and escape.

### Anchor discourses indexed
- SN 36.6 — first arrow / second arrow:
  physical pain does not require secondary mental suffering;
- SN 36.10 — feeling is rooted in contact and ceases with the corresponding contact;
- SN 36.16 — feeling analyzed by definition, origin, cessation, path, gratification, drawback, escape;
- SN 36.31 cross-linked through satipaṭṭhāna materials — worldly and non-worldly forms of pleasant/painful/neutral feeling;
- MN 9 cross-link — feeling -> craving in the causal chain;
- SN 45.169 cross-link — the Eightfold Path is developed for direct knowledge/full understanding/abandoning of feelings.

### Reasoning architecture extracted
- PRIMARY_SECONDARY_SUFFERING;
- CONTACT_ROOTED_AFFECT;
- FEELING_AS_CRAVING_HINGE;
- GRATIFICATION_DRAWBACK_ESCAPE;
- TRAINED_NON_REACTIVITY_WITHOUT_NUMBING.

### Important synthesis
The path does not aim at:
- never feeling pain;
- suppressing feeling;
- equating equanimity with absence of sensitivity.

It aims to transform the causal relation:
**feeling -> craving/aversion/appropriation.**

### Translation risks
- vedanā;
- sukha / dukkha;
- upekkhā in relation to neutral feeling vs trained equanimity;
- sāmisa / nirāmisa;
- anusaya.

### Phase-1 result
**STRUCTURAL_CORE_STRONG / FEELING-REACTION MODEL ACTIVE**

## 6. SN 46 — Bojjhaṅga-saṃyutta

### Structural core
The seven awakening factors describe a dynamic development sequence:
- mindfulness;
- investigation of principles;
- energy;
- rapture;
- tranquility;
- samādhi;
- equanimity.

They are:
- grounded in ethics;
- developed under conditions of seclusion/dispassion/cessation;
- directed toward letting go;
- integrated with mindfulness practice and liberation.

### Anchor material indexed
- SN 46.1 — the seven factors are developed grounded on ethics and ripen as letting go;
- SN 46.36 — development leads to growth/progress;
- MN 118 / SN 54.13 cross-links — mindfulness of breathing and satipaṭṭhāna fulfill the awakening factors in a causal-developmental sequence;
- MN 2 / AN 4.14 cross-links — awakening factors are an active development method for abandoning defilements.

### Reasoning architecture extracted
- DEVELOPMENTAL_SEQUENCE;
- BALANCED_ENERGY_AND_CALM;
- ETHICS_AS_FOUNDATION;
- MINDFULNESS_TO_INVESTIGATION_TO_STABILITY;
- LETTING_GO_AS_DIRECTION.

### Important synthesis
The awakening factors reinforce that early Buddhist meditation is not passive observation.
It contains:
- remembering/maintaining the object/teaching;
- investigation;
- active energy;
- affective transformation;
- calming;
- collectedness;
- equanimity.

### Translation risks
- bojjhaṅga;
- sati;
- dhammavicaya;
- viriya;
- pīti;
- passaddhi;
- samādhi;
- upekkhā;
- vossagga.

## 7. Cross-block architecture

The first five priority blocks now form one integrated map:

### SN 12 — conditional engine
How suffering and release arise through conditions.

### SN 22 — appropriation engine
What is taken as "I/me/mine" and how grasping generates bondage.

### SN 35 — experience interface
How faculty + object + consciousness -> contact -> affective/cognitive process.

### SN 36 — hedonic hinge
How feeling becomes craving/aversion or can be known without the second arrow.

### SN 45/46 — training engine
How the path and awakening factors actively transform the causal process.

### Combined model
**CONDITIONS -> EXPERIENCE -> FEELING -> APPROPRIATION/CRAVING -> SUFFERING**
can be interrupted through
**RIGHT VIEW -> EFFORT -> MINDFULNESS -> INVESTIGATION -> CALM/COLLECTEDNESS -> RELEASE.**

This is a functional synthesis.
It is not a claim that every sutta explicitly presents the entire chain.

## 8. Contradiction-hunting consequences

The current structural map rules out these simplifications:

- dependent origination is only about three-life rebirth;
- aggregates are merely a psychology taxonomy;
- sense fields imply metaphysical idealism;
- feeling means modern "emotion";
- mindfulness is passive nonjudgment;
- samādhi alone is the path;
- right view is merely intellectual belief;
- awakening factors are independent meditation tricks.

Remaining active tensions:
- exact rebirth-transition mechanism;
- metaphysical scope of "the all";
- exact historical status of some technical path taxonomies;
- relation between Aṭṭhakavagga rhetoric and systematic right-view language;
- sequence/interaction of samādhi and insight across genres and practitioners.
