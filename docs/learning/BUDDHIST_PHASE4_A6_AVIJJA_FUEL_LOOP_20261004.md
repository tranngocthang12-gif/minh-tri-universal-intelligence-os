# PHASE 4 — A6 — AVIJJĀ, FUEL, AND THE SELF-REINFORCING DEPENDENT-ORIGINATION LOOP

**Status:** CHAT_TAUGHT / PARTIAL_SOURCE_AUDIT  
**Date:** 2026-10-04

## Question

If consciousness and name-and-form are dependently conditioned, where does ignorance operate, and what keeps dependent origination going?

## Primary-text findings

### SN 12.2 — bounded source-audit slice
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: ignorance is defined as not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path leading to its cessation.
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: with ignorance as condition, formations; with formations, consciousness; with consciousness, name-and-form; then the six sense fields, contact, feeling, craving, grasping, becoming, birth, aging-and-death.
- This closes only a bounded definitions slice of the outstanding SN 12 audit. The full SN 12 ID-level backlog remains open.

### MN 9
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: right view repeatedly analyzes a phenomenon through the pattern: phenomenon, origin, cessation, and path.
- The received Pāli presentation includes a mutual relation between ignorance and the taints; parallel-text placement differs, so no larger historical claim is promoted from that feature alone.
- `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS`: ignorance is not adequately modeled as mere lack of memorized propositions; right view is transformative understanding that changes the causal process.

### SN 12.52
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: attending to gratification in things that can fuel grasping grows craving; attending to their drawbacks supports craving's cessation.
- `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS`: the causal loop is sustained not only by the presence of objects and feelings, but by how experience is taken up and valued.

### SN 12.64
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: where passion, delight, and craving for the four nutriments are present, consciousness becomes established/grows; name-and-form alights, formations grow, and renewed becoming follows.
- Where passion, delight, and craving are absent in that relation, consciousness does not become established/grow in that way and the renewed-becoming sequence does not develop.
- Do not paraphrase this as the blanket claim "consciousness ceases to exist."

### SN 12.23
- `TEXT_ATTESTED`: concentration conditions truly knowing-and-seeing, which conditions disillusionment, dispassion, freedom, and knowledge of ending.
- `CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS`: wisdom interrupts the loop by changing the way conditioned experience is understood and therefore what receives craving/grasping as fuel.

## Milindapañha dialectical layer

- `MILINDAPANHA_DIALECTIC`: Nāgasena describes wisdom as both severing and illuminating; the lamp analogy says wisdom dispels the darkness of ignorance and makes the Noble Truths evident.
- Use this to stress-test, not replace, the early-text definition of ignorance.

## Main conclusion

`CROSS_TEXT_SYNTHESIS`:
Ignorance is not best understood as a simple missing fact in a mental database. It is a failure to know-and-see suffering, its origin, cessation, and path in the operative way required by the path. In the dependent process, feeling can become craving, craving fuels grasping, and passion/delight/craving provide conditions in which consciousness becomes established and renewed becoming develops. Wisdom is the counter-movement: knowing-and-seeing → disillusionment → dispassion → freedom.
