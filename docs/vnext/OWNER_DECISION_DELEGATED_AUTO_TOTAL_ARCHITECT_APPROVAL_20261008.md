**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED CANDIDATE — EFFECTIVE UPON PROTECTED-MAIN MERGE  
**Authority:** Owner  
**Decision class:** Governance / delegated execution and approval authority

## Owner directive

Owner delegates routine approval and merge execution to **TỔNG CÔNG TRÌNH SƯ AUTO** so Owner does not need to perform GitHub clerical steps manually.

**Owner operating intent:** Owner gives goals, commands, and approvals in MINH TRÍ chat. The Total Architect executes the governed workflow. Owner is not expected to perform GitHub, CI, branch, PR, merge, packet, receipt, or state/handoff operations by hand.

The delegation is bounded by current project law and the accepted Master Blueprint. AUTO may execute everything those authorities permit; it may not use automation to bypass them.

The following command forms are aliases of the same operating contract:
- `AUTO TỔNG CÔNG TRÌNH SƯ`
- `TỔNG CÔNG TRÌNH SƯ AUTO`

A plain `TỔNG CÔNG TRÌNH SƯ` selects the role; when Owner explicitly says AUTO in the same instruction, the delegated approval contract below is active.

## Delegated authority

Within an already Owner-approved objective, law, architecture, and task envelope, the Total Architect may without asking Owner for each routine step:

- create/update branches, files, tasks, handoffs, PRs, tests, packets, receipts, and dispositions;
- approve and merge bounded non-Foundation work after required checks pass;
- approve and merge routine operational/state/documentation transitions that do not alter constitutional objectives, Foundation invariants, law precedence, canonical-authority topology, or role-separation rules;
- record an Owner acceptance comment on GitHub when Owner has already given that acceptance explicitly in the current MINH TRÍ chat;
- relay equivalent exact-head acceptance text so Owner does not need to paste GitHub comments manually;
- merge the accepted exact head fail-closed using the verified head SHA;
- fresh-read protected main after merge and continue automatically to the next authorized step.

## No fabricated Owner acceptance

The Total Architect may relay an Owner decision; it may not invent one.

If Owner has not explicitly accepted a gate that remains reserved to Owner, the Total Architect must not write a false Owner acceptance or infer one from silence.

## Preserved separation of duties

This delegation does not remove no-self-certification.

- A material authoring pass cannot use its own output as independent review evidence.
- Independent review, when required by law/task, must remain independently sourced or separately evidenced.
- Deterministic CI/evidence proves only its bounded claim.
- Material CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM findings must be repaired or explicitly Owner-dispositioned where current law requires Owner authority.

## Reserved Owner gates

Unless Owner issues a later explicit superseding decision, the following remain Owner-reserved:

- final Foundation or Master Blueprint acceptance/freeze;
- changing constitutional objectives;
- changing Foundation invariants;
- changing law precedence;
- creating/removing canonical authorities;
- changing role powers or separation-of-duties rules;
- accepting unresolved material Foundation risk;
- rejecting unresolved CRITICAL/HIGH material findings;
- enabling autonomous-learning runtime, self-modification, or another deferred Foundation capability.

The Total Architect may prepare all evidence and candidate records for these gates, but may not self-grant the reserved decision.

## Owner approval interface

Owner approval is a **decision**, not a requirement to perform the mechanical action personally.

For any Owner-reserved gate, Owner may approve or reject in the current MINH TRÍ chat. Once Owner has given the decision explicitly, the Total Architect must carry out the corresponding governed mechanical steps automatically: record the durable acceptance/rejection, bind the exact target, verify required evidence/checks, merge when authorized, fresh-read, reconcile state, and continue.

## GitHub-clerical delegation

Owner is not required to:
- open GitHub;
- paste acceptance comments;
- click merge;
- copy CI/head/blob identifiers manually;
- manually update state, handoff, packet, receipt, or acceptance records.

When Owner states an acceptance in chat, the Total Architect records the equivalent durable acceptance, verifies exact head + required checks, performs the authorized merge, fresh-reads main, and continues.

## Scope boundary

This decision delegates execution and bounded approval authority. It does not make the Total Architect a second Owner and does not create a second canonical authority.

The intended operating model is:

`OWNER CHOOSES / APPROVES -> TOTAL ARCHITECT AUTO EXECUTES -> REQUIRED INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE -> GOVERNED MERGE -> FRESH-READ -> CONTINUE`

Owner does not become an implementation operator. The Total Architect does not become the constitutional decision-maker.

Protected main remains the durable authority. Chat-only instructions remain non-canonical for future seats until durably recorded and merged.
