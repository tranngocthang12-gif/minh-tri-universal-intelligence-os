# OWNER DECISION — MASTER BLUEPRINT FIRST — 2026-10-06

**Status:** OWNER FOUNDATION DIRECTIVE / CANDIDATE UNTIL PROTECTED-MAIN MERGE

Owner directs that MINH TRÍ must no longer evolve by local chat interpretation or patch-by-patch architecture growth.

From this decision forward, foundation work follows:

`MASTER DESIGN -> LAW -> BUILD -> SUPERVISE -> VERIFY -> ACCEPT -> OPERATE -> CONTROLLED CHANGE`

The organization and its law are designed together from the start. Neither law nor structure is built in isolation:
- constitutional invariants and authority boundaries constrain architecture;
- architecture makes those laws executable and recoverable;
- changes to either are governed by the same protected lifecycle.

Before Foundation Freeze may resume, the project must have one project-wide Master Blueprint that defines:
- final system purpose;
- stable foundation vs extensible zones;
- law hierarchy and conflict resolution;
- canonical source-of-truth map;
- role separation and approval authority;
- lifecycle from design through operation;
- cold-start recovery for new AI/chat/model seats;
- change classes, review, validation, acceptance, and reopen rules.

Required roles:
- Owner;
- Architect;
- Builder/Implementer;
- Supervisor/Inspector;
- Independent Reviewer/Critic;
- Evidence/Validation layer.

Separation of duties:
- Architect may not self-accept architecture.
- Builder may not self-certify implementation.
- Supervisor must compare implementation to approved design.
- Independent critic must be able to challenge design and implementation.
- Evidence/Validation must bind claims to machine-checkable or independently reproduced evidence.
- Owner remains final authority for foundation decisions.

Foundation Freeze is held until the Master Blueprint and its governance model are reviewed, accepted, made canonical, and shown recoverable by a fresh seat.
