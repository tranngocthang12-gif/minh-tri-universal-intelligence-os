# PROJECT LAW v0.2 — RESTORATION DELTA CANDIDATE

**Status:** `PROPOSED DELTA / NOT ACTIVE LAW`

This file proposes additions to `PROJECT_LAW.md` after the Owner-provided historical architecture was reconciled with current repository evidence. It does not replace or outrank `PROJECT_LAW.md` until explicitly accepted.

## Proposed invariant additions

16. **Understanding is demonstrated, not asserted** — reading/repetition is not enough; material understanding must support explanation, differentiation, prediction and/or falsification appropriate to risk.
17. **Learning must mature beyond memory** — the system may track demonstrated maturity from Memory through Transfer; no level is claimed without scoped evidence.
18. **Learning must produce reusable skill when justified** — validated lessons may become versioned procedure/skill candidates only after benchmark and promotion gates.
19. **The learning method is itself testable** — meta-learning must compare procedures against baseline, including error, cost, time and transfer.
20. **Repair must be sandboxed and reversible** — self-repair cannot silently mutate Owner mission, Project Law, risk authority or external-action permissions.
21. **Route by demonstrated capability, not provider brand** — provider/model selection should be based on task-fit evidence and scorecards; no permanent AI ownership.
22. **Work the highest-leverage eligible bottleneck** — do not maximize research/activity; decompose the Owner target and select the material gap most likely to improve the decision/outcome.
23. **Architecture expansion requires a proven bottleneck** — do not add subsystems merely because they are intellectually attractive.
24. **Tier separation is explicit** — Tier 1 learns/understands/governs; Tier 2 owns domain knowledge; Tier 3 executes under separate capability gates.
25. **Transfer method, not unsupported conclusions** — cross-domain transfer applies to learning procedure and validated abstractions, never to domain claims without new evidence.
